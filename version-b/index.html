Getting started

How Fernhall works

Three steps, and the first one takes about two minutes.

  1. 1 🔗

    Link your bank account.

    Sign in to your bank through a secure connection. Fernhall can see your transactions and balance. It cannot move money out of your account without a transfer you have approved.

  2. 2 🧮

    We find your safe amount.

    Fernhall looks at your last three months of income and spending, then suggests a monthly amount. You can raise or lower it before anything moves.

  3. 3 🌱

    Watch it add up.

    On the first of each month, your amount moves into your savings account. You can see your balance and history at any time.

Good to know

Questions, answered

  • 💸

    Can Fernhall take money I need?

    Fernhall only moves the amount you've approved, and it leaves room for your bills and the month ahead. You can pause or change the amount at any time.

  • 🔐

    Is my bank login safe?

    You sign in through your bank's own secure connection. Fernhall never sees or stores your bank password.

  • 📈

    How much can I save?

    That depends on your income and spending. Fernhall suggests an amount based on your last three months, and you decide whether to use it.

  • 💰

    What if I want my money back?

    You can withdraw from your Fernhall savings account at any time. There are no lock-in periods and no withdrawal fees.
