Your journey starts here

How the magic happens

Three simple steps, and the first takes about two minutes. Let's dive in.

  1. 1 🔗

    Connect your bank, seamlessly

    Securely sign in through your bank. Fernhall can see your transactions and balance — but it's not able to move money without a transfer you've approved.

  2. 2 🧮

    Discover your safe amount

    Fernhall analyzes your last three months of income and spending to suggest a monthly amount. You're always in control — raise or lower it before anything moves.

  3. 3 🌱

    Watch your savings grow

    On the first of each month, your amount moves into your savings account. Track your balance and history anytime — your financial journey, at a glance.

Got questions? We've got answers

Everything you need to know

  • 💸

    Will Fernhall take money I need?

    Absolutely not. Fernhall only moves the amount you've approved, always leaving room for your bills and the month ahead. You're in full control — pause or change it at any time.

  • 🔐

    Is my bank login secure?

    Yes, completely. You sign in through your bank's own secure connection, so Fernhall never sees or stores your password.

  • 📈

    How much can I save?

    It depends on your unique financial situation. Fernhall suggests an amount based on your last three months — and you decide whether it's right for you.

  • 💰

    Can I withdraw my money?

    Of course. Withdraw from your Fernhall savings account at any time, with no lock-in periods and no withdrawal fees. Your money, your rules.
